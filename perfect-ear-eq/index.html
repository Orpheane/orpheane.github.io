Frequently asked questions

Questions? Answered.

What is Perfect Ear EQ?

Perfect Ear EQ is a free Chrome browser-tab audio equalizer with six presets, a 7-band EQ, optional Auto EQ, compression and up to 600% boost for quiet audio.

Is Perfect Ear EQ free?

Yes. It is free to install from the official Chrome Web Store and has no ads.

Can I boost quiet audio in Chrome?

Yes. The volume boost reaches up to 600%, depending on your source and playback equipment. A look-ahead brickwall limiter helps prevent output clipping. Loud recordings may reach the output limit at lower levels.

Which presets are available?

Music, Bass Boost, Voice, Cinema, Soft and Custom. Use left and right arrows to browse without changing your current sound until you select a preset.

Will my custom EQ settings be saved?

Yes. Custom remembers your EQ settings and your independent Auto EQ and compressor choices across preset switches and when reopening the extension.

Does it change sound everywhere on my computer?

No. Perfect Ear EQ processes audio in the Chrome browser tab you choose, not system-wide desktop audio.

Does it collect personal data?

The developer states that no personal data is collected or transmitted, settings and diagnostics stay on your device, and no external requests are made.

Do I need audio experience?

Not at all. Start with one of the presets, then explore the seven bands or enable Auto EQ whenever you like.
